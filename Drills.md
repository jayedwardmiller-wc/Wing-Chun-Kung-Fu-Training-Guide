### <a id="drills_eyes"></a> Eye Training

There are six muscles in the eye, two in the middle and 4 around. They connect to the central nervous system.  
The goal in eye training is to enhance your visual acuity allowing you to see and react much quicker. 

Warm-up  
Train the eyes by following an object in six directions (side to side, up and down, and diagonal) and then focus on the object coming within six inches your face.

Thumb circles (Clockwise (20)) (Counter Clockwise (20)  
Index Finger Up and down (20)  
Index finger left and right (20)  
Left hand index finger Diagonal (20)  
Right hand index finger Diagonal (20)

Proximal-Distal Eye Training   
Finger to nose and back out (20)  
From 45 degree diagonal to cheek and back out (left and Right (20))  
Nose around to temple (Left and right (20)

Peripheral Training  
Hold Thumb arm fully extended to:  
Front of face then moved still extended to the side. (Left and Right (20)  
Clock (10 then 5\) (2 then 8\) (12 then 6\) Hold 10 seconds each.
