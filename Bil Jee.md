## THRUSTING FINGERS &emsp;(BIL JEE) &emsp;     ![][image1]
### Description
Bil Jee, also known as Biu Jee, depending on linage preference, is the third and final empty hand form practiced in Wing Chun Kung fu. Bil Jee introduces additional wing chun footwork building on the footwork introduced in Chum Kil. It has highly dynamic, multi-directional, and aggressive movements. It breaks traditional Wing Chun rules such as keeping the elbows tightly tucked by extending the range of motion and incorporating intense spinal rotation and waist-driven power. 

The movements are fast and explosive, utilizing the fingertips, forearms, and elbows to create a "vortex" or "whirlpool" effect that can either violently fling external force away or suck an opponent into a counter-attack. It contains short bursts of coordinated arm movement during short-range elbow attacks and long-range finger strikes.  This form teaches coordinated double kicking techniques. It is divided and taught in eleven sections.
### Purpose
Nicknamed Gow Gup Sau (Emergency Hands). While the first two forms teach you how to maintain and defend your centerline, Bil Jee assumes you have made a critical error, your defenses have collapsed, your guard is breached, or you have been pinned/trapped. Its core purpose is to recover lost centerline control, structure, and balance from a severe disadvantage. It functions as a tactical safety net, providing a last resort to turn a losing situation back into an advantageous one.
### Benefits
- Short-Range Explosive Power (Ging): Trains the body to generate massive "inch energy" or explosive power through tight, compact distances.
- Unlocks the body's full biomechanical potential by driving movement outward from the hips through the shoulders. This forms a central "gyroscope" or core axis, allowing you to project explosive force smoothly in any direction.
- Enhanced Footwork & Agility: Introduces Huen Ma (Circling Stance), which allows for rapid, safe changes of direction to evade attacks and unbalance opponents at severe angles.
- Extreme State of Relaxation: Teaches the practitioner to maintain a completely tensionless body state until the absolute split-second of impact, maximizing potential force.
### Check Points
- When executing a thrusting hand (Bil Jee) concentrate on getting energy to the tips of the fingers. The wrist pivots at the point of contact while the opposite elbow must draw back symmetrically bringing the opposite hand back to Wu Sao position anchoring the movement. The extending arm must lock cleanly at its peak to prevent overextension and joint vulnerability.
- When executing the elbow strikes, either keep the fists together at centerline or place fist in Wu palm. Power must explicitly generate from the rotation of the waist/hips while your feet remain firmly rooted to the ground. Do not allow your weight to drift or lean out of balance when extending your range.
- No matter how wild, angled, or off-center an emergency technique looks, every movement must ultimately return to and recover the center axis of the body.
- The practitioner starts and ends the form standing on the same spot on the floor.
#### Stances and Foot Work
1. Neutral stance (Yee Jee Kim Yeung Ma)
2. Neutral side stance (Peen Sun Ma)
3. Forward stance (Gung Ma)
4. Circular step (Huen Bo)
5. Front facing side stance (Chiu Ying Ma)
6. T – stance (Ding Jee Ma)
7. Parallel side step (Waahng Bo)
#### Hand/Arm Techniques
1. Thrusting fingers (Bil Jee)
2. Circling Hand (Huen Sao)
3. Palm Strike (Jeung)
4. Grabbing Hand (Larp Sao)
5. Jerking Hand or Suffocating Hand (Jut Sao)
6. Arm Break (Jip Sao);
7. Thrusting Hand (Bil Sao)
8. Double Palm Strike or Butterfly Palm Strike (Po Pai Jeung)
9. Splitting Block- Combination of Jom Sao or Pac Sao for one hand and Gan Sao or Low Fut Sao for the other hand <!-- Gan or Fut depending on height and attack defended -->(Kan Sao or Gaang Sau)
10. Rotating Arms or Weaving Hands - Combination of Tan Sau and Bong Sau (Quan Sao)
11. Double Arm Grab (Seung Sau Laap)
#### Kicking Techniques:
11. Front Kick then Side Kick (Zing Gerk then Zaak Gerk)
12. Inside Stomp Kick then Front Kick (Woey Booi Gerk then Zing Gerk)

###  <a id="bj_comm"></a>Commencement
1. From the ready position with your tongue touching your upper palate, train your eyes at a far distance, concentrate, and breathe normally.
2. Raise both arms up to shoulder level, with palms down.
3. Clench hands into fists.
4. Turn your fists palm side upward.
5. Chamber your fists below your armpits by pulling them toward your body, keeping them as high as possible without touching your body.  This opens up the lungs.
6. Flex your knees, squatting down slightly to lower your center of gravity and improve your stability.
7. Swing your left foot in an arc forward and out to the left side placing it parallel to the right foot at a distance just wider than shoulder width apart.
8. Swing your right foot in near to your left.
9. Swing your right foot forward and out to the right side forming a neutral stance.
10. Open your hands, position them in front of your chest, palms towards chest.
11. Extend your arms forward and down, crossing them left over right, belt height. This establishes both the centerline and the border between lower gate and middle gate.
12. Fold your arms in front of your chest wrists crossed with the right arm on the outside, palms open at eye level. This establishes the center line and the boundary between middle gate and high gate.
13. Clench your hands into fists.
14. Chamber your fists back to their original positions beside your body.

### <a id="bj_verthori"></a>Vertical and horizontal finger thrust (left)
15. Execute a left internal gate punch from the heart (left noy moon chung)
16. Open the left hand, holding it vertically in preparation for bil jee.
17. Execute the following:
18. Downward bil jee;
19. Upward bil jee; 
20. Downward bil jee; and
21. Upward bil jee.
22. Turn hand to a horizontal position.
23. Execute the following:
24. Outward bil jee;
25. Inward bil jee;
26. Outward bil jee; and
27. Inward bil jee.
28. Execute left heun sao, rotating the wrist clockwise flexing the wrist moving hand up and inward towards center line, downward and continuing around.
29. Clench your left hand into a fist
30. Chamber your fist back to the side of your body

###  <a id="bj_kansao"></a> Kan Sao (left)
143. Prepare to execute a left kan sao (Splitting Block - Combination of Right Jom Sao and Gan Sao)
144. Step to a left neutral side stance and execute a left kan sao
145. Step to a right neutral side stance and execute a right kan sao
146. Step to a left neutral side stance and execute a left kan sao 
147. Bring the left foot to the right (not touching floor) then execute a left front kick 
148. Bring kick back (not touching floor) and pose left bon sao while executing a left side kick 
149. Place down into a right neutral side stance while executing a left rotating huen sao with right hand at elbow (supporting)
**Alternate footwork for 146-149** 
>146. Step to a left T – Stance, right foot behind left foot, and execute a left kan sao.
>147. Execute a left front kick.
>148. Bring kick back and pose left bon sao while executing a left side kick.
>149. Return to the T – Stance and step to right neutral side stance while executing a left rotating huen sao with right hand at elbow (supporting)
150. Step to a left neutral side stance and execute a left jut sao (facing right) with right at elbow (supporting)
151. Step to right neutral side stance while executing a left rotating huen sao with right hand at elbow (supporting)
152. Step to a left neutral side stance and execute a left jut sao (facing right) with right at elbow (supporting) 
153. Step to a right neutral side stance while executing a left rotating huen sao with right hand at elbow (supporting)
154. Step 45° forward to the left with the right foot
155. Continue step while doing left pak sao and right dai jeung
156. Step back to the right with the right foot to a neutral stance
157. Right fut sao to side
158. Raise the right arm then bring down to centerline jut sao with the left wu sao supporting the right elbow
159. Execute a right pak sao while swinging left foot in circle to neutral stance and left dai jeung from above pak sao.
160. Execute a left huen
161. Clench your hands into a fist
162. Chamber your fists back to the side of your body

### <a id="bj_futsaobilsao"></a>Fut Sao and Bil Sao (left)
207. Step to a left neutral side stance prepare left hand for fut sao
208. Circle Step to left foot to left front stance while executing a left fut sao
209. As Fut Sao returns to center line Wu Sao with the right hand is  slightly below and under left elbow in preparation for a right Tsuen Sao  
210. Thrust right Tsuen Sao to right Bil Sao while stepping to right front stance. 
211. While remaining in right front stance execute left Tsuen Sao to Bil  left Bil Sao, and then a right Tsuen Sao right Bill Sao 
212. Face left in neutral stance
213. Right jing jeung or optionally Right Bil Jee with left hand Wu Sao supporting right elbow
214. Face center and execute a right fut sao while circle stepping back to a neutral stance.
215. The right hand executes a Jut Sao while left circle stepping back to a neutral stance and thrusting a left tsuen sao to Bil Jee from below.
216. Execute a left huen sao
217. Clench your hands into a fist
218. Chamber your fists back to the side of your body
### Fut Sao and Bil Sao (right)
219. Step to a right neutral side stance prepare right hand for Fut Sao
220. Circle Step to right foot to right front stance while executing a right Fut Sao
221.  As Fut Sao returns to center line Wu Sao with the left hand is  slightly below and under right elbow in preparation for a left Tsuen Sao  
222. Thrust left Tsuen Sao to left Bil Sao while stepping to left front stance. 
223. While remaining in left front stance execute right Tsuen Sao to  right  Bil Sao, and then a left Tsuen Sao left Bill Sao 
224. Face right in neutral stance
225. Left Jing Jeung or optionally left Bil Jee with right hand Wu Sao supporting left elbow
226. Face center and execute a left fut sao while circle stepping back to a neutral stance.
227. The left hand executes a Jut Sao while right circle stepping back to a neutral stance and thrusting a right Tsuen Sao to Bil Jee from below.
228. Execute a right huen sao

### <a id="bj_torsobend"></a> Torso bend
247. Bend the torso down and thrust double vertical bil jee to the floor
248. Swing the arms up crossed at wrists left over right as the torso straightens up
249. Straighten the torso continuing arm swing high and around to sides then forward ending in a left Man Sao and right Wu sao.
250. Execute a left Noy Moon Chung
251. Cycling fists to a right Noy Moon Chung  and keep it extended
252. Execute a left Noy Moon Chung and keep it extended 
253. Execute a double huen sao 
254. Clench your hands into a fist
255. Chamber your fists back to the side of your body
256. Bring the left foot to the center and bring right foot to center, stand upright arms at your side.
